Cookie Policy

What we store in your browser and why. Updated 15 October 2026.

1. What cookies and local storage are

A cookie is a small file that a website stores on your device. Equivalent technologies, such as browser local storage (localStorage and sessionStorage), are used alongside them and are subject to the same rules.

We do not use advertising or profiling cookies or social media pixels, and we do not share the measurement with third parties.

3. Our own visit measurement (only if you accept)

If you click "Accept", we anonymously record which drawsports.app pages are visited, what kind of source the visit comes from (for example, social media or a search engine), the campaign parameters, the language, and whether the App Store or purchase link is clicked. We do not record your name or email, and we do not store your IP address in the events. The data is stored on our systems (Supabase, European Union) and web measurement events are deleted after 13 months. If you click "Reject", no identifier is stored and no measurement event is sent.

4. Legal basis

Strictly necessary technologies are exempt from prior consent (art. 22.2 LSSI-CE). Our own visit measurement is enabled only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Cookies that Paddle sets during checkout are governed by its own cookie policy.

5. How to change your choice

You can change it right now: change my cookie preferences. You can also block or delete storage from your browser settings; note that disabling technical storage may affect checkout.
