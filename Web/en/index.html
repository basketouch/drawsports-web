High-performance logic, for ANY sport.

Although I come from basketball, visual analysis is universal. DrawSports includes official courts and fields for Futsal, Handball, Volleyball, Hockey and more. If you use video to improve, this is your app.

Adaptable to your discipline: Configurable courts and fields.

⚡️

Quick Video Analysis

Import from iCloud, Drive or USB and start in seconds. Mark cuts while watching the game: each action becomes a clip automatically. And with smart history, pick up where you left off without wasting time searching.

❄️

The "Freeze Frame" Effect

Don't just draw: explain. Use the zoom for detail or create a Freeze Frame: freeze the image, draw the tactical correction, and decide exactly how many seconds that pause lasts. Turn a video into a lesson.

📋

Tactical Whiteboard Pro

Design plays and concepts without needing video. Switch between Notebook mode for quick notes or use Official Courts for the real court context in your explanations.

🚀

Project and Share

In the video room: connect to the TV and keep total control (speed, frame by frame). To send? Export loose clips or a full sequence, choose whether to include audio or drawings, and send via WhatsApp or AirDrop instantly.
